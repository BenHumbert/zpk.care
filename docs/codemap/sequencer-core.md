What `proof.verify()` is here: `StateTransitionProof` is an o1js `Proof` subclass (src/engine/state-transition.ts). o1js `Proof.verify()` sets the instance's `shouldVerify` flag (`Bool(true)`); the flag is consumed only when that proof object is passed as a private input to a ZkProgram method, where Pickles verifies it inside the circuit. Invoked outside a circuit (seq:293; node:989) it performs no cryptographic check and does not throw for a structurally valid proof. Out-of-circuit verification in o1js needs `verify(proof, verificationKey)` or `Program.verify(proof)`; no file under src/ or visualizer/ imports `verify` from `o1js` (grep), and the only `.verify(` call sites in the core are seq:293, node:989, bp:91-92, 131-132, 191-192, cp:80, 143-144. (o1js semantics stated from the o1js Proof API; node_modules not opened per reading rules.) The main process does hold a StateTransitionProgram VK once the engine compiles (engine:977) but never uses it for submitted proofs.

Consequence: the first cryptographic check of a submitted StateTransitionProof is in-circuit inside `BatchProgram.wrapSingle`/`wrapPair` (bp:91-92, 131-132) when a batch is proved — in a pool worker (worker:105-116) or, without a pool, in the main process (seq:553-562). By then steps 10-18 have mutated trees, roots, audit chain and claim accumulator, returned a receipt, and gossiped it to replicas (node:522-548). On proving failure the drained transitions go back to the front of the pool (seq:591; seq:902-907 via node:1500, lev:1011); the failing proof is not identified or dropped.

## Open questions

- The WAL, quorum, heartbeat/failover and `L2Leviathan` code paths are unreachable from `start-leviathan.ts` (it builds `L2Node`, never calls `setWal`/`enableQuorum`); is a different launcher expected to use them, or are they dormant?
- `proof.verify()` flag semantics are stated from the o1js Proof API; node_modules was not opened. Confirm against o1js 2.13 `Proof.verify()` if the audit needs a direct citation.
- `enrichAuditEvent` (seq:2403) and `applyAdminRootUpdate` (seq:1324) have no callers in rpc, src/nodes or the engine (grep) — dead or called from an unmapped file? **`enrichAuditEvent` answered by D5 (2026-10-18): dead, removed.**
- `batchVerificationKey` (node:311) is stored and never used; was `verify(proof, vk)` intended at node:989?
- Is `ZKTRIDENT_GOSSIP_SECRET` required to be hex (node:350 `Buffer.from(seed, 'hex')`)? If not, the attestation seed silently shortens.
- Nothing in seq/node removes a proof that fails in-circuit at batch time from `pendingPool`; what is the operational recovery for a poisoned pool in l2Only mode?
- `exportState().registeredWallets` is derived from receipts (seq:2833-2840): after restart, admin-provisioned (receipt-less) wallets and wallets whose receipts were evicted (seq:926-934) are missing from the rebuilt trees and checks are relaxed (seq:3059-3062). Is that the intended recovery contract?
- `atypes:34` documents an 8-field commitment; `audit:64-77` hashes 10 fields with `patientPayCents`/`planPayCents` always 0 (never populated). Which is canonical for external verifiers?
- `storeMessage` accepts any `MessagePayload` as "ciphertext" (msg:75-108); whether callers actually encrypt belongs to the messaging map.
- `adjudication-state.json` content (engine:5640-5710) is written by this launcher but owned by the engine map; its member fields need a decision under I1/I5/I6.
