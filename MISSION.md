# Mission

This software was built to solve **the hidden waste, secrecy, and privacy
violations baked into how prescription-drug claims are priced and adjudicated**
for **patients, the employers who sponsor their benefits, and the pharmacists
and prescribers who serve them** — and it's being given to the world freely, so
that no patent, paywall, or corporate gatekeeper can ever lock it away.

It is released under the Apache License 2.0. Anyone — individuals, nonprofits,
or companies — may use it, build on it, and even sell products based on it.
There are no restrictions on commercial use. That openness is intentional: the
more people who adopt and improve this technology, the more people it reaches,
and the harder it becomes for anyone to claim ownership over what should belong
to everyone.

But openness comes with a hope, and an ask.

This project was not built to make anyone rich. It was built to help. So while
the license places no limit on what you may charge, the intent behind it is
clear: the people who ultimately rely on this technology should be treated
fairly, not exploited. If you build a business on this work, I ask you to:

- Price your product around the value you genuinely add, not around what
  desperate people can be made to pay.
- Be transparent that your product is built on free, open technology.
- Contribute improvements back to the community when you can.
- Reach out — good-faith partners are welcome here, and I'd be glad to
  recognize those who honor this intent.

I built this because I believe useful things should be shared, and because the
time I have is better spent helping people than guarding a patent. If this work
makes even one person's situation a little easier, it will have been worth it.

Use it well. Build something good with it. And keep it fair.
