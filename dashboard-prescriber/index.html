Step 1: Accept Enrollment Token

Enter the enrollment token provided by your employer or group administrator.

Step 2: Generate Enrollment Key

Generate a cryptographic enrollment key. This key is created on your device and never transmitted.

Step 3: Derive Wallet (NPI Credentials)

Enter your prescriber credentials. The wallet ID is derived via Poseidon hash. No private keys involved — access is proof-gated only.

1 = NPI, 2 = DEA, etc.

Step 4: Enroll

Register your wallet with the sequencer. The insurer sees only your opaque wallet ID — no credentials or private data are transmitted.

Step 5: Compile ZK Programs

Compile the zero-knowledge circuits. Generates verification keys and prepares the prover. Typically takes 3-5 minutes.
