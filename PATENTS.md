# Patents

**This project is not encumbered by any patent held by the author.**

A patent application covering aspects of this work was filed, but the author
has chosen to let it lapse rather than pursue or enforce it. No patent has
issued, and none will be sought.

Instead, this software is released openly as a **defensive publication**. By
publishing the full implementation, the techniques it describes become prior
art — they remain free for everyone to use and cannot later be patented and
locked away by anyone else. That open release, not a patent, is what keeps this
technology free.

**About the license text:** the Apache License 2.0 (Section 3) contains a
patent-license grant from each contributor. Because the author holds no patent,
that clause conveys no patent rights on the author's behalf — it is simply part
of the standard, unmodified Apache 2.0 text and is left exactly as written.

*This document is a plain statement of intent, not legal advice.*
